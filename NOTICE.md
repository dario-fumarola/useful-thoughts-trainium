# Private research handoff

This bundle combines user-supplied source/context with newly written helper code
and documentation. It is intended for the owner's existing research workspace.
No new public license is assigned to the supplied legacy material. Original
notices and authorship remain in force. Model/dataset licenses are separate and
must be checked before acquisition/use. No cloud credentials, private keys,
model weights, raw training corpus, or paid-service access is bundled.

Do not publish this repo, its historical internal notes, or generated checkpoints
without the owner's instruction and applicable rights review. The project-only
transcript excerpt intentionally omits unrelated private conversation.
